How it works

1. Earn balance in REWARDS by clicking, or buy skins in the Shop tab.

2. Select skins and/or balance on the left, then choose a more expensive skin on the right.

3. Chance = your value ÷ target price × 95%, from 1% to 75%. The arrow stops on a random point: inside the yellow arc you win the skin, outside you lose what you put in.

4. Prices are real: the median of actual Skinport sales over the last 30–90 days, converted to rubles at the current USD rate and updated every 15 minutes. The balance is virtual and has no real-money value.
